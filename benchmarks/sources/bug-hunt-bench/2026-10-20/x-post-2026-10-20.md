I repeated DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 1017 benchmark 89 times across 9 harnesses to
understand which works best. High effort, 5 attempts each:

- Pi harness: 40.4
- Grok Build CLI: 40.4
- DeepSeek: 39.2
- OpenCode 2.0: 38.6
- OpenCode: 36.6
- Claude Code: 36.4
- Codex: 33.2
- Hermes Agent: 32
- Kilo Code: 31.8

Codex kept failing several times exceeding the allowed time per repo (3 hours) and
was judged where it finished. Pi, Grok Build CLI, and OpenCode 2.0 look like the
strongest universal harnesses in this run. The author explicitly says the result does
not necessarily generalize.
