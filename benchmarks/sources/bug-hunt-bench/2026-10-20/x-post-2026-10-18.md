Turns out DeepSeek harness improves the score of DeepSeek V4.1 Flash by 2-3 points on
average. It also costs 80% more due to subagents and more output tokens.

DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 1017 + Claude Code harness:
- max, mean of 7: 39.1
- high, mean of 5: 36.4
- low, mean of 5: 34.4

DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 1017 + DeepSeek harness:
- max, mean of 7: 42.7
- high, mean of 5: 39.2
- low, mean of 5: 34.8

The author states that all results are available on the benchmark website.
